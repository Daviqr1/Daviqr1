## 📖 The Origin Story

It all started when I was 12 years old. Driven by curiosity, I built an Android app that entirely digitized my school's ecosystem. That early leap into technology didn't just earn my name on a city billboard—it secured me a full academic scholarship until graduation and ignited a lifelong obsession with solving complex, real-world problems through code.

Fast-forward to today: I am a 24-year-old **Engineering Manager & Full-Stack/IoT Architect** specializing in bridging the gap between digital ecosystems and physical hardware. Whether I'm dealing with **Precision Agriculture, Industrial Automation, or GovTech**, I build systems that interact with the real world at millimeter precision.

## 🚀 Epic Quests & Engineering Impact

### 🚜 Precision Agriculture & Autonomous Systems @ **EMFLORA**
*Manager & PL Full-Stack / IoT Developer*
- **Autonomous Navigation:** Engineered and successfully validated the integration of custom-built **RTK Rovers & Base Stations** directly into tractors, achieving fully autonomous, millimetric field navigation.
- **Proprietary Hardware/Software:** Architected end-to-end proprietary IoT tech stacks from scratch, bypassing off-the-shelf limitations.
- **Computer Vision (AI):** Deployed state-of-the-art **YOLOv12** models for real-time area mapping and precise seedling differentiation using complex computer vision algorithms.

### 🏛️ GovTech Digital Transformation @ **Universidade Federal de Goiás (UFG)**
*Manager & PL Mobile Developer*
- **Public Sector Scale:** Leading the architectural overhaul and mobile development of high-traffic governmental platforms, bringing enterprise-level performance, security, and reliability to portals used by citizens nationwide.

### 🏭 Industrial Software Architecture @ **Own Enterprise**
*Founder & Lead Tech Architect*
- **Mission-Critical Rust:** Designed, built, and deployed ultra-reliable, mathematically validated PLC (Programmable Logic Controller) software entirely in **Rust** for heavy industry giants like **Brametal**. This ensures memory safety, extreme performance, and zero-downtime execution in harsh manufacturing environments.
